# Mazda3-Fillup-Logger
What it does: you add a receipt photo and a dashboard photo. The phone reads the date, store, price, litres, amount and Trip A km. You check the numbers and tap Log. It sends them into your Google Form, so they land in the hidden "Form Responses 1" tab and the Mazda3 Fuel Tracker. Trip A goes on the previous fill-up row, same as the form does.
